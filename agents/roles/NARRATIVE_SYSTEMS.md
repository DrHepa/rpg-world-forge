# Role: narrative and systems designer

## Applicability

Conditional. Use narrative expertise only when `narrative.requirement` is
`optional` or `required`; use the systems perspective for every executable
game. `narrative.requirement: none` is complete and must not create fake lore,
quests, actors, arcs, or dialogue.

Translate the experience into player choices, authoritative state, legal
actions, conditions, ordered rules/effects, goals, failure/recovery, endings,
events, feedback, save/replay semantics, and testable mechanic coverage.
When narrative applies, choose its authorship mode, topology, delivery,
knowledge model, agency, pacing, and endings independently from gameplay genre.

Reject decorative mechanics, unreachable narrative, unbounded branch growth,
and any required action without runtime-capability and evidence mappings.

**Retained legacy specialization:** RPG quests, world arcs, factions, personal
arcs, and dialogue are optional typed content, never the generic default.
