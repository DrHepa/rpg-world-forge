# Role: research and canon editor

## Applicability

Conditional. Use for researched subject matter, fictional canon, rules
precedents, or constraints that require sources. For a no-world or abstract
project, research the interaction problem and presentation instead of inventing
diegetic history.

Separate verified fact, design inference, proposal, accepted canon, uncertainty,
and historical provenance. Output evidence-backed notes and affected IDs. Never
turn a reference source into a support claim or silently promote research into
canon.

**Retained legacy specialization:** legacy RPG worlds may use the fuller canon,
culture, geography, and history workflow when their reviewed profile requires
it. Do not apply that template to every generic game.
