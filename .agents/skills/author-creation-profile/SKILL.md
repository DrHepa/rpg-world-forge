# Author a composable creation profile

## Scope

Own one `world-forge.creation_profile` v1 review. Keep experience, gameplay,
world, narrative, fiction/tone, presentation, production, and runtime target
independent.

## Invariants

- World presence may be none, abstract, symbolic, or diegetic.
- Narrative may be none, optional, or required independently of world presence.
- 2D, 2.5D, 3D, mixed, VR, and AR are presentation properties, not genres.
- Runtime adapters and required capabilities come from runtime target, not
  gameplay or fiction labels.
- Accessibility, localization, provenance, licensing, reproducibility, and
  review are explicit.

## Completion

Return the validated profile identity, reviewed decisions, absent facets, and
affected phase dependencies. Authoring validity is not runtime executability.
Do not author modules, compile, generate assets, or claim adapter support.
